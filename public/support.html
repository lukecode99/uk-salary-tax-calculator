Common questions

Which tax year does it use? Pick the tax year in Settings; the calculator applies that year's thresholds and rates.

Are my figures sent anywhere? No — everything is calculated on your device. See the privacy policy.

The numbers differ from my payslip. Payslips vary with tax code, student loan plan, salary sacrifice and mid-year changes. Check those in Settings first; if it still looks wrong, please open an issue with the inputs you used.
