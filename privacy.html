UK Salary & Tax Calculator

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 July 2026

Your salary and tax data

Never leaves your device. Every calculation runs locally. Salary figures, pension contributions and anything else you type are not collected, transmitted or shared — there is no server that could receive them.

Advertising

The app displays a banner advert supplied by Google AdMob. To serve and measure it, Google's SDK collects technical data from your device, such as your IP address, device and operating-system information, and whether the ad was seen or tapped. None of the figures you enter in the app are shared with Google or with advertisers.

Ads are requested as non-personalised. That means they are not selected using a profile of your interests or browsing history, and the app does not ask for permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites. Google may still use the technical data above for frequency capping, fraud prevention and aggregated reporting.

Google's handling of that data is governed by Google Privacy & Terms and AdMob's data disclosure.

Analytics

None. The app contains no analytics or crash-reporting service.

On-device storage

Preferences such as your tax year, hours per week and pension settings are stored locally on your device. Deleting the app removes them.

Children

The app is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

Any material change to this policy will be published here with a new date above.
